05

Account deletion terms

Please read the following information carefully. This document explains what account information and account-related activity may be removed when you continue.

01 — Your account

When you delete your QytModels account, the account associated with the current browser session will be removed from the local QytModels prototype. This means that the website will no longer recognize the account as an active account after the deletion process has finished.

The account is identified by information such as the username and email address that were entered during registration. Once the account is removed, this information will no longer be available through the account system used by this prototype.

02 — Username and profile information

Your username is part of your QytModels identity. Deleting the account removes the username from the account record stored by this prototype. The profile area will no longer display the deleted account after the deletion is completed.

Any future profile information connected directly to the account should be considered part of the account and may also become unavailable when the account is deleted. The website does not promise that deleted account information can be restored later.

03 — Email address

The email address used by the account is also stored as part of the account record. During deletion, the account record containing that email address is removed from the local prototype.

After deletion, the website will no longer use that account record to identify you as a logged-in user. Deleting the account therefore means that the current account identity is removed rather than simply hidden from the interface.

04 — Password information

The password itself is not stored as plain text by this prototype. Instead, the current prototype stores a cryptographic hash derived from the password. Deleting the account removes the account record that contains this password data.

After deletion, the deleted account cannot be used to log in through the account record that existed before the deletion. A future account registration would be a separate account.

05 — Likes

Likes associated with the current account are stored separately in this browser. These saved like states are connected to the account email and the individual model identifier.

When the account is deleted, the prototype removes the saved like information associated with that account. Your previous personal like state should therefore not be expected to remain available after deletion.

06 — Ratings

Ratings submitted by the current account are also stored locally by this prototype. A rating represents the value selected by the account for a particular model.

When the account is deleted, the locally stored rating information associated with the account is removed. This means that you should consider your personal rating history unavailable after deletion.

07 — Favorites and saved content

QytModels may contain features such as favorites, saved models, collections, bookmarks or other forms of personal organization in the future. Information that is directly associated with an account should be considered potentially removable when that account is deleted.

Even if a feature is not yet active in the current prototype, this section exists to explain the general principle: deleting an account is different from simply logging out. Logging out keeps the account. Deleting the account removes the account itself.

08 — Uploaded models

If QytModels later allows users to upload models, scenes, textures, materials, previews or other files, content associated with an account may have additional deletion rules. Users should always check the current website information before deleting an account that contains uploaded content.

The current local prototype does not operate a real online model storage service. Therefore, this prototype cannot provide a guarantee about future server-side content systems that may be introduced later.

09 — Reviews and comments

Future versions of QytModels may include comments, reviews, discussions or other public contributions. Content created by an account may have separate rules once a real online service exists.

The current prototype does not provide a complete server-side review system. This section is included because account deletion should be treated as a permanent action and not as a temporary hiding action.

10 — Download history

Future versions of QytModels may record information about model downloads, viewed models or other interactions. Such information may be associated with an account depending on how the future service is implemented.

Deleting an account means that you should not rely on being able to recover a personal history connected to that account later.

11 — Settings and preferences

Account-related settings and preferences may also be removed when the account is deleted. This can include personalized interface settings, saved preferences, collections and other information introduced in later versions of QytModels.

Logging out does not have the same effect. Logging out is intended to temporarily end the current session, while account deletion is intended to permanently remove the account.

12 — Connections to future features

QytModels may grow over time and may contain additional features that are not present in the current prototype. An account may eventually be connected to galleries, creator pages, model collections, contests, comments, notifications or other services.

Because future features may change, you should never assume that an account can be restored with all of its previous information after deletion.

13 — Deletion is different from logout

Logging out simply removes the current login state. Your account remains available and you can log in again later using the correct credentials.

Deleting the account is a separate action. It removes the account record used by this prototype and removes the locally stored personal interaction data connected to that account.

14 — Recovery

There is no account recovery button in the deletion process. Once the final deletion button has been confirmed, the website does not provide a normal undo action.

If you are unsure whether you really want to delete the account, you should stop the process instead of continuing. You can close this window or choose the cancel option at the available confirmation stages.

15 — Browser storage

The current QytModels prototype stores account data using the browser's local storage system. This is not the same thing as a production online account database. The information belongs to this browser environment and does not automatically synchronize between devices.

Because of this, deleting the account in this prototype means removing the account information that the prototype stored in this browser. It should not be interpreted as a statement about a future production QytModels server.

16 — Other browser data

The deletion process removes account-related data that the current prototype can identify as belonging to the current account. It does not mean that every possible piece of browser information unrelated to QytModels is removed.

Your browser itself may have its own history, cache, temporary files and other information. Account deletion from QytModels should not be confused with clearing your entire browser.

17 — No promise of restoration

Once the deletion is complete, you should treat the account as permanently removed. Do not continue under the assumption that the previous account can simply be brought back.

If you later create another account using the same email address, that would be a new account and should not be assumed to contain the information from the deleted account.

18 — Final understanding

By continuing through this process, you acknowledge that account deletion is a permanent action within the current QytModels prototype. You understand that account information, personal interaction data and other account-related information may no longer be available after deletion.

You also understand that the current prototype is still under development and that its storage system is different from a future production website. Information about future features may therefore change as QytModels develops.
